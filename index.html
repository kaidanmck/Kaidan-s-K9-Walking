Description

Kaidan’s K9 Walking is a small dog walking business started by a responsible 14-year-old who loves dogs and staying active. My goal is to help busy dog owners make sure their pets get the exercise, fresh air, and attention they deserve. At Kaidan’s K9 Walking, every dog is treated with care, patience, and kindness. My slogan is “Happy Dogs. Happy Walks.” because I believe a good walk keeps dogs healthy, happy, and full of energy. Whether your dog needs a quick walk around the neighborhood or a longer adventure, I make sure they stay safe, have fun, and come back home.
